# Code of Conduct

Code Forge is a game for people who are learning to code, and many of the people who open an issue
here are beginners. Everyone should be able to ask a question, report a bug or suggest an idea
without being made to feel small.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being patient with beginners and explaining things plainly
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who play and build Code Forge

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Mocking someone for a "basic" question or a beginner mistake
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews)
and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, you can:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (this goes to GitHub's moderators), or
- open a new issue titled **"Private contact requested"** with no details in it. The maintainer
  ([JreyForFun](https://github.com/JreyForFun)) will reply with a private way to talk.

All reports are handled privately, and the maintainer respects the privacy and safety of the person
who reports.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For language or behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome: a private, written
   warning explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences
   for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban
   from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from the project.
