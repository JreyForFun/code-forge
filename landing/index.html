Rules at a glance

Hearts
Five at most. A wrong answer costs one; one refills every 5 minutes, and one is free each day.
XP
10 XP for a first clear, +5 if flawless. Every 100 XP is a new level.
Streak
Clear one lesson a day. A streak saver covers one missed day.
Leagues
Seven weekly leagues. Earn 100 XP in a week to move up.
